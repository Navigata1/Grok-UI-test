# Publish checklist

- [ ] Title final: 30 to 55 characters, promise inside the first 40, no thumbnail words repeated.
- [ ] Thumbnails A and B exported at 1280x720, under 2MB, graded "ship", two different concepts; a person turns Test & Compare on at upload.
- [ ] Description first line restates the promise in plain words; links after the fold.
- [ ] Chapters match the retention map beats.
- [ ] End screen points at the most related proven video, not the newest.
- [ ] Pinned comment asks the question the sequel will answer.
- [ ] Community post drafted and two Shorts cut from the best moments, each pointing at the video; a person schedules them in Studio after publish (the post inside 24 hours, the Shorts inside 48).
- [ ] Publish time matches when your returning viewers are online (Studio > Audience).
- [ ] The 48-hour review is on the calendar with the baseline numbers ready.

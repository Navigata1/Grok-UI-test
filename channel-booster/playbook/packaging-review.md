# Packaging review

The last gate before production. Ten minutes, two people, one question: would a stranger click this next to the best competing videos, and would the video keep the promise?

## Check

1. Say out loud what the title tells and what the thumbnail shows. If they are the same sentence, one of them is wasted.
2. Cover the thumbnail: does the title alone make a specific promise? Cover the title: does the thumbnail alone make you curious?
3. Put the pair in a mock feed next to the three strongest competing videos at phone size. Would you click yours?
4. Name the promise in one sentence. Is it on screen in the first line of the script?
5. Run `booster package review --title ".." --thumb-text ".."`. Title under 60 or overlap over two-thirds fails.

## Verdicts

- Pass: proceed to the story spine.
- Revise: one issue, fix it in the room, re-check.
- Fail: two or more issues, back to the packaging sprint. Do not "fix it in the edit".

## Record

Write the pair, the verdict, and the promise into the packaging ledger. The 48-hour review will need it.

# Post-mortem

Two reviews per video: 48 hours (decide) and 7 days (learn). Read the funnel in order; the first broken stage is the bottleneck.

| Stage | Metric | Healthy | Broken means |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Idea | impressions vs baseline | >= 2x your median views | the system found no audience: demand or channel-signal problem |
| Packaging | CTR vs baseline | >= 0.9x baseline and >= 3% | seen, not clicked: swap thumbnail, then title |
| Hook | 30-second retention | >= 60% | clicked, then left: the open did not deliver the promise |
| Retention | average percentage viewed vs baseline | >= 0.85x baseline | the middle sags: cut and re-hook |

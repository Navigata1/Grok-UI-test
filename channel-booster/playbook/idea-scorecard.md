# Idea scorecard

An idea is not a topic. An idea is a topic plus an angle plus a package you can already see. Score every candidate on six axes, 0 to 5, before anything is written or filmed.

| Axis | Weight | 0 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Demand | 25% | No evidence anyone wants it | Multiple outliers (>= 10x channel median) on the topic in the last 90 days |
| Packaging | 25% | Cannot picture the thumbnail | The thumbnail is obvious and the title writes itself |
| Fit | 15% | A different audience | The exact viewer of your last three videos clicks it next |
| Angle | 15% | A straight copy of the outlier | A twist that makes the original look incomplete |
| Payoff | 10% | The promise is bigger than the delivery | Payoff inside 60 seconds and escalating |
| Feasibility | 10% | Needs a crew and a month | Ships inside the normal cycle |

Verdict: 75+ green, 55 to 74 yellow, below 55 red. Demand or packaging at 1 or below is an automatic red. Any axis at 1 or below caps a green at yellow.

## Where demand evidence comes from

1. Outlier scan: `booster outliers competitors.csv`. A video at 10x its channel's median is the audience voting with their time.
2. Your own audit: `booster audit my-channel.csv`. Your 5x videos are the formats you have already proven.
3. Search and comments: recurring questions in your comments, "how do I", "is it worth", "what happened when".
4. Sequels: the strongest demand signal on the platform is a video that already worked. Brief the sequel before the new topic.

## The angle test

Write the outlier's title next to yours. If a viewer who watched the outlier would learn nothing new from your title, you do not have an angle yet. Angles that reliably work: a constraint (budget, time, tool), a stake (what you lose), a contrast (cheap vs expensive, then vs now), a first-person test (I tried), a contrarian claim (why the popular advice is wrong).

## Bank, do not bin

Yellow ideas go into the idea bank with their weakest axis named. Half of them turn green when a new outlier appears or the packaging clicks. Red ideas are recorded with one line on why, so nobody re-pitches them.

# Channel audit

Run quarterly, or before any strategy change.

1. Export your uploads (title, views, publish date, duration) and run `booster audit my-channel.csv`.
2. List every video at 5x your median or better. These are your proven formats: brief a sequel to each before trying a new topic.
3. List the bottom quartile. Write what their packaging has in common (generic titles, no face, no number, no stake). That list is your "never again".
4. Check format lift: the formats that over-index among winners are the ones to lean on for the next five videos.
5. Positioning in one sentence: who watches, what they get every time, why they come back.
6. Upload cadence: fewer, better-packaged uploads beat more uploads. If more than half your videos are below median with no packaging test, cut cadence and add a packaging sprint.
7. The next five videos: title + thumbnail concept for each, every one borrowing a proven format and adding an angle.

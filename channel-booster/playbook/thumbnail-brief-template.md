# Thumbnail brief

Fill this in before the shoot, not after the edit. The thumbnail photo is a shot on the shot list.

## Brief

- Idea:
- Title (final or leading candidate):
- Promise the thumbnail must make (one sentence):
- Focal subject (the one thing the eye lands on):
- Emotion (if a face is present, name it: shock, doubt, pride, fear, relief):
- Supporting element (at most one):
- Text (three words or fewer, never the title):
- Background (clean gradient, blurred location, flat channel colour):
- Colour pair (high contrast: yellow/black, white/red, orange/blue):
- Channel signature to keep (framing, colour, typeface):

## Five concepts to sketch

| Concept | Lever | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Result | payoff | The end state, big, with the creator reacting |
| The Stakes | fear of loss | The creator and the thing that could go wrong |
| The Curiosity Gap | unresolved image | The key detail hidden or circled |
| The Contrast | before / after | A split frame, same framing both sides |
| The Identity | belonging | The creator with the one object the audience recognises |

Sketch all five at phone size on paper. Produce the two that survive plus one wildcard.

## Rules

1. One focal subject.
2. At most three elements: subject, one supporting object or reaction, optional text.
3. Three words of text or fewer. The title tells; the thumbnail shows.
4. Thumbnail and title say different things.
5. High contrast between subject and background; check at 120px wide.
6. Faces need a specific, readable emotion that matches the promise.
7. Clean background. Clutter kills clicks.
8. Phone first: safe margins, no thin lines, no small text, nothing important bottom-right (timestamp).
9. One idea per thumbnail.
10. Consistent signature across the channel so returning viewers recognise you in the feed.

## QA before export

- Readable at 120px wide on a phone screenshot?
- Makes a promise the video keeps?
- Would you click it next to the top three competing videos?
- Subject separated from the background?
- Exactly one idea?
- Text three words or fewer and different from the title?
- 1280x720, under 2MB?

Score each variant with `booster thumbnail qa`. Ship only at grade "ship".

## Test plan

Test & Compare with A (strongest) and B (most different). Let YouTube pick on watch-time share, not CTR alone. If CTR rises and average view duration falls, the winner over-promises: fix the open, not the image. Log the result in the packaging ledger.

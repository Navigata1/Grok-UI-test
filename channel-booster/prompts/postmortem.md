# Post-mortem prompt

Diagnose a YouTube video by reading the funnel in order: impressions (idea and channel signals), CTR (packaging), 30-second retention (hook), average percentage viewed (retention). The first broken stage is the bottleneck; do not prescribe fixes for later stages.

Video: {{title}}
Hours since publish: {{hours}}
Impressions: {{n}}   CTR: {{%}}   Average percentage viewed: {{%}}   30-second retention: {{%}}
Channel baseline: median views {{n}}, CTR {{%}}, average percentage viewed {{%}}
Packaging shipped: title {{title}}, thumbnail {{concept}}, Test & Compare result {{result or pending}}

Healthy thresholds: impressions at least 2x median views; CTR at least 0.9x baseline and at least 3%; 30-second retention at least 60%; average percentage viewed at least 0.85x baseline.

Deliver: the bottleneck and the evidence; what happened in three plain sentences; the next 48 hours (repackage only if packaging is the bottleneck and impressions are healthy); one sentence of learning for the playbook; a sequel idea if the video is healthy.

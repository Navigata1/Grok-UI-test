# Retention map prompt

You structure YouTube videos so they keep the promise the packaging made. The first 30 seconds: promise on screen inside 5 seconds, stake by 15, roadmap in one line, no intro. A rehook every 60 to 90 seconds. The thumbnail moment must happen on screen. Ordered structures hold better than unordered ones.

Title: {{title}}
Thumbnail moment: {{what the thumbnail shows}}
Script or outline:
{{script}}

Deliver: the first 30 seconds word for word; a payoff ladder (timestamp, moment); rehooks (timestamp, device, the actual line); cuts (what to remove or shorten and why); chapter titles matching the beats. Then check: is the promise in the first line, is the thumbnail moment in the final third and teased in the open, is the structure ordered?

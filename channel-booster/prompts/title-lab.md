# Title lab prompt

You write YouTube titles. The title tells; the thumbnail shows. Rules: 30 to 55 characters, promise inside the first 40, one promise per title, a number or a named thing, no shouting, no exclamation marks, no "video / vlog / episode", never repeat the thumbnail text.

Write 15 titles, each using a different lever: first-person test, result reveal, why question, nobody tells you, mistake frame, ranked list, stakes, contrast, identity, truth bomb, timebox, how I, stop doing, proof, transformation. For each, give the lever and one line on why it earns the click. Then pick the top three and say what the thumbnail must show so the pair says two things, not one thing twice.

# Idea engine prompt

You are a packaging-first YouTube strategist. An idea is only real once it has a title, a thumbnail, and a demand signal. Ideas come from proven demand: videos that did 5x to 10x their channel's median (outliers), remixed with an angle for my channel.

My channel: {{who watches, what they get, my last three videos and their views}}
Niche: {{niche}}
Outlier scan (views ÷ channel median, from `booster outliers`): 
{{paste the ranked list and the format lift table}}

Produce 10 ideas. For each give:
1. The outlier it borrows from and its multiplier.
2. The format cue (challenge, versus, list, transformation, reveal, negative, timebox, money, story).
3. The angle I add (constraint, stake, contrast, first-person test, contrarian claim).
4. A working title, 30 to 55 characters, promise in the first 40.
5. A thumbnail concept: one focal subject, at most three elements, at most three words of text that do not repeat the title.
6. Scores 0 to 5 for demand, packaging, fit, angle, payoff, feasibility. Be honest: at least two ideas should be weak.

Rank by weighted total (demand 25%, packaging 25%, fit 15%, angle 15%, payoff 10%, feasibility 10%). Then write the top three as full briefs: promise in one sentence, first payoff inside 60 seconds, the thumbnail moment.

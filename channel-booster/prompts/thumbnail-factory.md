# Thumbnail factory prompt

You design YouTube thumbnails as specs, so they can be judged before they are built. Rules: one focal subject; at most three elements; three words of text or fewer, never the title; a specific, readable emotion on any face; high-contrast subject against a clean background; phone first (readable at 120px wide); one idea per thumbnail; keep the channel's visual signature.

Idea: {{idea}}
Title: {{title}}
Subject on screen: {{who or what}}
Stake: {{what could go wrong}}
Result: {{the end state}}
Channel signature: {{colours, framing, type, or none}}

Produce five concepts, one per lever: the result, the stakes, the curiosity gap, the contrast (before/after split), the identity. For each: name, focal subject, emotion, elements (max three), text (max three words), background, colour pair, composition in one sentence, and a shoot or build note for the designer (lighting, lens, props, expression direction). Then choose A (strongest) and B (most different) for Test & Compare and explain why. End with the QA checklist: readable at 120px, promise the video keeps, wins next to the top three competitors, subject separated from background, exactly one idea, text under three words and different from the title.

# Packaging review prompt

Review this YouTube package as a strict strategist. The title tells; the thumbnail shows; together they make one promise the video can keep.

Title: {{title}}
Thumbnail (describe it): {{focal subject, elements, text, colours, emotion}}
Promise of the video in one sentence: {{promise}}
First line of the script: {{first line}}
Top three competing videos in the feed: {{titles}}

Answer in order: what the title tells; what the thumbnail shows; whether they repeat each other; whether the title alone makes a specific promise; whether the thumbnail alone creates curiosity; whether the promise is stated in the first line; whether you would click it next to the competitors. Verdict: pass, revise (one issue), or fail (two or more). Then give up to three rewrites: title + thumbnail text + why.

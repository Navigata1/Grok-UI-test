## So what: design implications for the Channel Booster

1. Packaging comes first in the pipeline. The workflow generator gates production on a title and thumbnail concept that pass review (`booster workflow`, stages 1 and 2).
2. Ideas come from outliers. The idea engine starts with `booster outliers` and scores demand and packaging at half the total weight (`playbook/idea-scorecard.md`).
3. Thumbnails are specs before they are images: one subject, three elements, three words, high contrast, phone first, text that does not repeat the title (`booster thumbnail qa`).
4. Storytelling is packaging kept: the first 30 seconds state the promise, the thumbnail moment happens on screen (`playbook/first-30-seconds.md`).
5. The first upload can win, so a new channel runs the same system from video one, and a cold start is diagnosed the same way: impressions, then CTR, then the first 30 seconds, then retention (`booster postmortem`).
6. Fewer, better uploads: the cadence is one packaging sprint per publish, not a daily upload target (`booster cadence`).
